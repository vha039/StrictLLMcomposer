## Escalation

### Stage 1 — Terra Max to Sol XHigh

- Fundamentally ambiguous vacancy source.
- Strategically complex or heterogeneous hiring field.
- Use only for the affected cluster; do not change the default route.

### Stage 3 — Terra XHigh to Terra Max

- Complex portal package.
- Repeated genericity or completion problems.
- Several stretch jobs.

### Stage 4 — Sol XHigh to Sol Max

- High rewrite requirement.
- Competing candidate identities.
- Complex reframing or claim risk.
- Public or regulated selection regime.
