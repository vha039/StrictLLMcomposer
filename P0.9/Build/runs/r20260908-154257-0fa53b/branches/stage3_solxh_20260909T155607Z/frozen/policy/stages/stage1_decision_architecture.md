# Stage 1 — Decision Architecture

Produce one vacancy-specific strategy hypothesis. You author no CV or letter.
Follow the current bundle's identity, handoff and schema. Write only
write.allowed at write.return_paths; never include tooling-owned fields.
The three factual authorities alone establish candidate facts.

## Read order and source access
Read the vacancy first; inspect read.vacancy_pages when supplied, otherwise
the original whenever source inspection is required. Platform summaries,
JobMatch and tips are not employer requirements.
Read all three frozen files in read.authorities (WORK, EDU, OTHER) in full
before ranking evidence or choosing the strategy. This includes their global
ceilings and unresolved items; no digest or shortlist replaces that reading.
Digests and keyword indexes are navigation only, never a basis for selection
or a factual citation. Rank against the original records, including alternatives.
You may always open the original frozen authorities directly. evidence_ids
and evidence_fetch support precise references and re-reads; they are not an
access boundary. No reading budget, cap or discouragement applies.
Read read.baseline_map. Where useful, use its exact ref values in the optional
cv_direction.employment_focus and evidence_plan[].baseline_refs fields; do not
guess them or use BL as factual EV evidence. The inventory creates no duty to
populate an optional list. It is mechanical context, not a factual authority.
Read the canonical personality profile and facet index; voice is not an
input to this stage. Factual evidence cannot come from personality.

## Decision routing
A named route includes its subsections. Use:
- D Mål og myndighet/Ubetrodd vacancy-innhold for input roles;
- D Målhierarki/Yrkesanker for objectives and occupational identity;
- D Single Backbone og marginal argumentverdi/Diagnostisk signal/
  Integrert bevisenhet for selection and proof architecture;
- K CLAIM_STRENGTH/CONTRIBUTION_AND_TEAM/PROOF_FUNCTION_LIFECYCLE/
  MATERIAL_REQUIREMENTS_AND_GAPS for claims, ownership, state and requirements;
- D Komplementaritet mellom CV og søknad and the top-zone principle in
  Komplett CV-doktrine for allocation; D Begrunnet motivasjon for motivation.
D means WRITING_DOCTRINE.md; K means PERSUASION_CALIBRATION.md in writing_policy.

## Encode the actual decisions
Use primary_candidate_identity/headline_hypothesis for the recognizable
occupational anchor. employer_decision_problem/selection_logic describe the
employer's decision, distinguishing stated requirements from your inference.
shortlist_thesis/application_direction.backbone expresses the supported route.
Rank evidence by vacancy decision value, truth/ownership, proof function,
function-appropriate lifecycle, diagnostic value, category coherence and
document role. There is no global maturity ladder.

evidence_plan[].role states the supported proposition and the employer
decision it serves. It is a compact argument instruction, not a retelling
of the source or ready-to-paste letter prose. claim_ceiling states the
constraints live for that use; lifecycle records the source-supported state.
Do not fill missing actions, causes, comparisons or adoption history.
Same-record association does not prove a relationship. Cross-record judgment
retains separate source context and attribution within a common argument.
Name useful judgment positively; do not mark it Støttebevis, kort som støtte,
a third case or “not a third case.”

material_requirements retains every material minimum, important and desired
requirement using the existing importance/relation/note/evidence_refs fields.
objections handles real gaps. Adjacent evidence is not fulfillment of a
minimum. Choose affirmative, truthful transfer without inventing equivalence.
cv_direction allocates breadth and credible top signals; application_direction
allocates interpretation and carrying arguments. motivation_bridge connects
supported experience to the actual employer task.
Use counterfactual_route when a materially different supported route would
give the final writer a useful option; do not manufacture a weak alternative.
writer_guidance/avoid_guidance contain vacancy-specific decisions or traps,
not repeated universal rules. “Do not transfer PRJ-06 adoption to PRJ-04” is
a live warning if the proposed pairing makes it relevant; “do not fabricate”
is policy repetition. No length/count target applies to these fields.

## Personality
Personality is ON. Select zero to three facets from the closed JSON index;
zero is valid. Bind selection to factual EV evidence. Populate all six
expression fields: plain_language_label, intended_employer_interpretation,
vacancy_relevance, expression_ceiling, expression_guardrail and misread_risk.
facet_id/evidence_refs remain selection provenance in strategy; the six-field
packet is the sole downstream personality-conditioning input. Preserve
INTERNAL_ONLY/RISK_PATTERN and global anti-inference boundaries. No typology,
private token or unsupported personality-derived fact reaches employer text.

## Ownership and completion
The final writer may replace this route, select different evidence and ignore
any facet. Strategy is neither factual authority nor final-package authority.
Use current EV IDs, never CV_BASELINE as factual evidence. Baseline references
are mechanical only. Return schema-valid semantic fields and execute the
bundle's exact submission.command. Only accepted submission completes work.
For the next vacancy, obtain fresh next/context and reset identity and
reasoning. D Variasjon innenfor en klynge owns semantic variation; never carry
the last employer's premise or stock prose into the next job.
