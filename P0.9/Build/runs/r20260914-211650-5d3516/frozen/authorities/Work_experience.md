`Forstå -> Avgrense -> Strukturere -> Bygge -> Kontrollere -> Forbedre`

### 0.5 Utvalgs- og syntesekontrakt

For hvert valgt prosjekt:

1. Bruk `selection_value` mot arbeidsgiverens faktiske problem, ikke Vegards generelle tekniske dybde.
2. Bygg prosa fra kompatible `claim_atoms` og bevar ceiling.
3. Bruk `do_not_lead_with` mot teknisk imponerende, men rolleforvrengende åpning. Rekkefølgerådet i feltet kan revideres etter faktisk arbeidsgiverrelevans; de faktiske forbudene i samme felt står fast.
4. Direkte økonomi-/regnskapsnær erfaring er positiv bro for prosessroller. Ikke gjør fravær av regnskapsbyrå-, lønns- eller årsoppgjørserfaring til frivillig svakhetsprosa.

**Flerdimensjonal statuskontroll:** Les alltid minst fire akser separat: leveransestatus (`planlagt`/`pilot`/`aktiv utvikling`/`implementert`/`fullført`/`avviklet`), faktisk bruk/adopsjon, Vegards nåværende eierskap og hvilken effekt som er verifisert. En sammensatt status skal ikke presses inn i ett sterkere ord. Match verbet mot den svakeste aksen som er nødvendig for påstanden.

## 2. Arbeidsmodell og ledelsesrelevant atferd

### 2.1 Ende-til-ende-leveransemodell

1. **Clarify the actual decision problem.** Separate the underlying need from the requested report, formula or tool; identify users, stakes, definitions and consequences of error.
2. **Map the current system.** Inventory data, files, code, models, transformations, manual steps, dependencies, ownership and limitations.
3. **Define architecture and source of truth.** Establish grain, keys, fact/dimension boundaries, calculation logic, assumptions, constraints and unresolved questions.
4. **Prioritize and decompose.** Separate decision-critical work from useful extras; define work packages, acceptance criteria and stopping rules.
5. **Implement or integrate.** Build directly or coordinate bounded coworker/AI-assisted implementation using the most suitable tool.
6. **Validate.** Use reconciliation, time-aware holdouts, leakage checks, differential/parity tests, code/workbook review, sensitivity analysis or other proof.
7. **Translate for the recipient.** Produce decision notes, dashboards, workshops, presentations, onboarding and technical documentation.
8. **Design for adoption and handover.** Expose assumptions, document logic, train users and define ownership/limitations so others can maintain the result.

### 2.2 Evidens for funksjonell ledelse

Safe signals:

- takes ownership of clarification, progress and the next actionable step;
- prioritizes decision-critical work and identifies dependencies/risks;
- structures multidisciplinary delivery and integrates contributions;
- aligns technical depth and communication with the stakeholder;
- facilitates workshops, challenges established approaches and transfers knowledge;
- creates onboarding, handover and inspectable documentation so others can use or maintain the result;
- supports adoption through controller enablement, supervised user observation, cross-organizational teaching and shared-method development;
- maintains delivery context, documentation, controls and explicit distinctions between adopted practice and proposal;
- makes uncertainty and limitations visible.

Use `teknisk/faglig pådriver`, `funksjonell/teknisk lederrolle`, `teknisk arkitekt`, `hovedimplementør`, `integrator`, `koordinerte` or `tok ansvar for` when supported. Do not convert this evidence into formal project-manager, product-owner, programme-owner or line-manager titles.
