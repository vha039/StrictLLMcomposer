Are you our new Senior Manager Digital Governance Operations?

As our new Senior Manager Digital Governance Operations, you will join Norwegian's newly
established Group Digital & Regulatory Governance function within IT & Business Services,
based at Fornebu, and take a central role in building and running our Group-wide capability for
digital and regulatory governance across a complex international organisation.

Reporting to the VP Group Digital Governance & Regulatory Affairs, you will be the senior
operational lead for the function's prioritised governance and regulatory matters. You will help
turn regulatory requirements and governance priorities into practical ways of working and drive
coordinated implementation across the organisation.

A key part of your role will be to bring structure and momentum to cross-functional matters,
ensuring relevant stakeholders are involved, the necessary information is gathered, agreed
actions are followed up and matters are escalated when necessary.

Our scope includes GDPR and privacy, NIS2, Digitalsikkerhetsloven and related digital security
regulation, the EU AI Act and AI governance, supplier and third-party governance, EASA Part-IS
interfaces and other emerging digital regulation relevant to the Group.

As we build this capability, you will also have a real opportunity to shape how we operate and
support the organisation in practice.

In this role, you will:

• Run and continuously improve the function's governance processes, working methods and
follow-up structures, identifying recurring issues and turning them into better guidance,
clearer ownership and more scalable solutions that reduce reliance on key individuals.

• Translate regulatory requirements and governance priorities into practical roadmaps,
implementation plans, processes and follow-up structures, maintain a structured view of
key requirements and implementation needs across prioritised areas, and lead selected

We believe you will thrive in this role if you:

• Have relevant higher education in law, business, technology, risk management or a related
field, or equivalent relevant experience.

• Bring solid experience from one or more relevant areas such as governance, regulatory
affairs, compliance, risk, business operations, advisory or transformation, ideally from a
complex or regulated organisation. Experience with areas such as GDPR, NIS2/
Digitalsikkerhetsloven, AI governance or other digital regulation is an advantage.

• Have strong analytical skills and a track record of getting to the heart of complex or loosely
defined matters, identifying what is needed and establishing clear next steps.

• Have experience translating requirements or strategic priorities into workable processes,
roadmaps, implementation plans or cross-functional initiatives, and understand how
regulation, accountability and governance translate into effective execution and follow-
through in complex organisations.

• Are comfortable working with dashboards, systems and digital tools and can turn complex
information into clear and useful management reporting and decision support, while
identifying opportunities to improve how work is structured and delivered.

• Communicate confidently with senior stakeholders and are comfortable asking for the
information you need, challenging constructively where needed and moving matters
forward without relying on formal line authority. You combine sound judgement with
strong execution skills and are hands-on, independent and structured, with the maturity to
balance pace, quality and stakeholder considerations.
