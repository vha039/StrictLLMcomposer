# Personality facets (closed set; choose at most 3)

Pick a facet only when the evidence shows the behaviour. Show the behaviour as conduct in the argument; never name a facet or its label in a document.

- **PF-01** Systems mapping & causal decomposition - Starts by mapping definitions, actors, dependencies, constraints, source authority, failure modes, incentives, and leverage points; prefers causal/first-principles explanations.
- **PF-02** Exploratory breadth & option generation - Explores broadly, generates alternatives, compares counterfactuals, and expands option space before converging.
- **PF-03** Reversible action & evidence loops - Acts at moderate certainty when action is reversible and informative; requires stronger evidence for irreversible/high-stakes decisions.
- **PF-04** Craft / integrity / usefulness-driven standards - High standards arise primarily from craftsmanship, epistemic integrity, and usefulness rather than fear of mistakes or criticism.
- **PF-05** Governance-based control & verified delegation - Seeks clarity over objectives, standards, evidence, interfaces, checkpoints, and rollback—not personal execution of every task.
- **PF-06** High autonomy without reflexive opposition - Evaluates advice by source, reasoning, stakes, and context; resists coercion or illegitimate authority but is not reflexively oppositional.
- **PF-07** Promotion / expansion motivation - Primarily pulled by opportunity, mastery, learning, capability expansion, and upside. QA/privacy/audit/rollback are guardrails, not core motives.
- **PF-08** Cognitive empathy / perspective taking - Defaults to understanding other people's beliefs, constraints, incentives, causes, and remedies; emotional accompaniment is less automatic, not absent.
- **PF-09** Precision-first evidence-backed communication - Values literal interpretation, explicit assumptions, direct disagreement, evidence, and concrete definitions.
- **PF-10** Rapid domain learning / practical competence acquisition - Strong tendency to enter unfamiliar domains, research aggressively, map them quickly, and become practically competent.
- **PF-11** Leverage, toolmaking & durable-improvement orientation - Seeks disproportionate durable benefit from well-designed interventions; enjoys converting complexity into usable models, tools, frameworks, and decision support.
- **PF-12** Auditability / source-of-truth orientation - Distinguishes source-of-truth state from narratives about state; favors saved artifacts, tests, diffs, evidence, checkpoints, and inspectability.
- **PF-18** Structured chaos: exploration with engineered convergence - Broad nonlinear exploration is followed by deliberate structure, consolidation, verification, and polish. J-like order is often engineered scaffolding around a strongly exploratory P-style mind.
- **PF-19** Pro-change / experimentation stance with production guardrails - Pro-change and pro-experimentation; negative results retain information value. Production caution reflects validation discipline, not generalized change aversion.

Not employer-expressible: PF-13, PF-14, PF-15, PF-16, PF-17, PF-20.
