## 1. Independent verdict

**The base plan is substantially correct and should be implemented, with one material amendment, three additions and four scope corrections.**

BP independently reproduced the two factual defects, correctly rejected the preceding instance's over-strong root-cause hypothesis, resisted every rejected mechanism, and produced edits that are genuinely decision-complete. Its Phase A verdict of PARTIAL is right, and its refusal to certify unobservable process claims from artifacts is the correct epistemic posture. I verified its central claims against disk rather than accepting them; the ones I checked held.

Where it fails, it fails in one direction: **it occasionally solves an authority-boundary problem by weakening a good writing instruction.** That is the specific failure this pass was told to guard against. Three of its five proposed `FORFATTERSTEMME.md` edits trade instructional force for a qualification that buys nothing, because the actual ambiguity lives in only two of the five locations. Amending that is my single most consequential change.

Its second limitation is scope, not quality: BP was not asked about the two operational expansions and does not address them. Two of its statements would obstruct them if inherited literally. Those are reconciled in §5.7 below and specified in UO.

I did not find a reason to reopen the settled semantic design, and I found no additional material falsehood in the two named finals beyond the ones BP already names plus one it under-rates (D5 below).

## 2. Adjudication of BP's substantive recommendations

### D1 — unsupported PRJ-04 workshop/training activity → **ACCEPT**

Independently verified. `Work_experience.md` §4.4 (`PRJ-04`, the section spanning the selection metadata through the claim atoms) contains **zero** occurrences of `workshop`, `Workshop`, `opplær`, `training`, `teaching` or `onboarding`. Its supported contribution is exactly four lines: four-person co-development; research synthesis, methodological improvement, economic/strategic analysis, governance and standardization; hospital-wide implementation; reported active approximately 2.5 years later. The wrong output is `T/jobs/H/current/final_package.json` body paragraph 0: *"Vi tok den videre gjennom workshops og opplæring i fagmiljøene som skulle bruke den."*

Workshop, onboarding and cross-organisational training evidence is real and abundant — in `PRJ-01`'s internal BI workshop, `PRJ-08`'s workshop planning and facilitation, `PRJ-02`'s technical training and §5.2. It belongs to those records. Attaching it to `PRJ-04` invents a shared history between records.

**Failure scenario, concrete.** Stage 2 is composing the Huseierne letter. `R04` is a `MINIMUM/GAP` on practical OKR and facilitation of goal processes. The carrying unit is `PRJ-04`. The writer needs a facilitation bridge, the integration rule says the judgment must be about the carrying case itself, and so the facilitation is written *into* `PRJ-04` rather than cited from `PRJ-08`. Output: an employer-facing sentence asserting an activity the candidate did not perform on that project, inside the one paragraph the employer is most likely to probe at interview.

Effect on application quality: this is the worst class of defect the system exists to prevent, and it is worse than an overclaim of scale because it is a fabricated event, not a stretched adjective. It is not defensible at interview.

**BP's fix is correct but its demonstration is incomplete.** BP supplies a truth-bound replacement that drops the workshop sentence entirely. That is safe but concedes the facilitation bridge the Huseierne argument actually wants. The discriminating repair keeps both:

~~~text
Rammeverket ble innført som sykehusdekkende standard og var fortsatt i bruk
om lag to og et halvt år senere.

Workshopfasilitering og onboarding har jeg gjort i annet arbeid, blant annet
da analyseavdelingens arbeidsformer ble etablert.
~~~

The first sentence is the adoption evidence; durability *is* the proof that a shared method took hold, and it needs no invented workshop. The second is lawful cross-record use under the corrected integration rule: separate context, own attribution, serving the same employer decision. Nothing is weakened, nothing is hedged, and the OKR-adjacent bridge survives intact. **This is the demonstration BP should carry**, because it proves the correction does not cost the argument.

### D2 — unsupported comparative and reinterpreted detail → **ACCEPT, with a sharper repair**

Independently verified. `Work_experience.md` §4.1 supports supervised tests with actual users, observed *confusing interactions*, collected feedback and focused improvement sprints. `T/jobs/H/current/final_package.json` body paragraph 1 says: *"Det som endret løsningen mest var likevel ikke modellarbeidet, men at jeg satte meg ned med faktiske brukere og så hvor de leste tallene annerledes enn vi hadde tenkt."*

~~~text
Jeg tok ikke for gitt at løsningen virket fordi den var levert. Jeg kjørte
veiledede tester med faktiske brukere, så hvor den var forvirrende i bruk,
~~~

Every element is documented. The persuasive work is done by the candidate's own decision and action rather than by a comparison the source cannot support. The employer still learns the point that mattered — this is someone who verifies adoption by watching use — without a manufactured ranking. **Add this alongside BP's version as the second analytical demonstration.**
