## Required decisions

### Must-fix before the two-job smoke

1. Complete GATE-10 with the actual model, effort, surface, file-access, and privacy posture.
2. Remove manual semantic-hash reconstruction from the operator/model path.
3. Wire current failure records and fix `next --stage 3`.
4. Make finalize/render/status reject or expose stale semantic/render state.
5. Ensure frozen schemas, masters, settings, and render reports are actually used and verified.
6. Make the PDF fallback unambiguous.
7. Create the pilot-log template before the first job.
8. Fix the known CV page-balance issue.

### Cheap high-value refinements

* Emit a single human-readable handoff from `freeze`, `next`, and `context`.
* Show all important CHECK reasons and limitations in the queue.
* Amend the Stage 2 claim-ceiling wording.
* Add a plain-language final review/submission checklist.
* Use relative output paths in operator views while retaining absolute paths internally.

### Best left to the pilot

* Whether Stage 1 requirement coverage becomes structured filler.
* Terra versus Sol for native Bokmål quality, effort, and human repair.
* How often Stage 3 materially reconstructs rather than tightens.
* Personality facet usefulness versus trait injection.
* Cross-job wording convergence within clusters.
* False `READY`, `CHECK`, and `RERUN` rates.
* Whether total human handling actually fits 10–20 minutes.
* Any Q_happy or model-quality conclusion beyond raw scores and qualitative notes.

### Positive deviations to preserve

* Run-local frozen inputs and immutable child branching.
* Vacancy-first Stage 1 with no fit score or apply/no-apply veto.
* EV/BL namespace separation and explicit claim/lifecycle ceilings.
* Dynamic CV zones combined with full baseline chronology.
* CV/documentation versus letter/synthesis complementarity.
* Stage 3’s real semantic freedom and permission to ignore upstream strategy.
* Evidence-bound 0–3 personality handoff with Stage 3 suppression.
* No regex-based language policing and no runtime quality/confidence score.
* Independent semantic, triage, and render axes, including valid degraded-PDF states.
* Human ownership of final review, external submission, and all irreversible actions.
* Targeted master repair, Word COM rendering, semantic hash exclusion of finalization metadata, and no-silent-overwrite behavior.
