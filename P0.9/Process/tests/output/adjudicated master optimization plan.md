### What was verified, and what “verified” means

The live files, original vacancy, relevant DOCX text and OOXML, run manifests, frozen authorities, strategy objects, render receipts and actual consumers were inspected before the rulings in Step 2. A matching path or quote does not prove the proposed causal explanation. The occurrence table verifies the locator and gives a source excerpt; the subsequent claim table adjudicates the factual inference made from it.

Every explicit numeric source citation occurrence in the plans is indexed below, including repeated uses and inherited `:line` references. Additional named fields, binary selectors, historical sources and absence claims are covered in the next table. New replacement text in a proposal is not misclassified as existing source text. O/P followed by a number identifies the **line in the input plan**, allowing its complete quoted claim to be recovered without confusing it with a framework line.

**Verified** = the cited source passage exists at the stated location; its interpretation still needs the claim table. **Drifted** = the intended passage exists elsewhere and the corrected locator is supplied. **Wrong** = the named object, quotation or factual inference fails inspection. **Unverifiable** = the evidence does not establish the claim; it is excluded from load-bearing use. Excerpts use “ / ” between source lines and “…” for omitted text.
