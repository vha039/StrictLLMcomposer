# P0.9 candidate architecture

> **Status:** best-supported current candidate for the Step-1 architecture freeze. It is **not** the formally adopted P0.9 release. Explicit user decisions and unsuperseded P0.8 rules are stated as such; cross-source choices remain `[SYNTHESIS]`, `STRONG_DEFAULT` or `[OPEN]`. See `01_CURRENT_STATE_AUTHORITY_AND_DECISIONS.md` for the authoritative decision ledger and resolution owners.

## 0. Candidate in one page

Jobbsøk_26 P0.9 should be a fresh, local, file-authoritative, manually initiated production system for one candidate. The durable semantic route remains:

```text
human-approved, normally frozen vacancies
        |
        v
deterministic intake + source/identity validation + run freeze
        |
        v
Stage 1 — Sol-class Decision Architecture
        |
        v
Stage 2 — Terra-class Native Package Writer
        |
        v
Stage 3 — Sol-class Elastic Final Writer + Selective Review
        |
        v
deterministic finalization, baseline merge, DOCX/PDF rendering,
expected-set reconciliation and derived review queue
        |
        v
human factual/semantic approval and manual submission
```

The system protects factual authority upstream while intentionally moving **strategic decision authority downstream**:

```text
FACTUAL AUTHORITY — fixed
candidate authorities + original vacancy
    > strategy, draft, final, personality and context views

STRATEGIC AUTHORITY — migrates downstream
Stage 1 hypothesis -> Stage 2 realized draft -> Stage 3 final decision -> human approval
```

This resolves the apparent conflict between strict schemas/provenance and an elastic final writer. The final writer may reconstruct identity, evidence emphasis and prose; it may never reconstruct reality. [SRC-06: §§10–16; SRC-08: §§1–7; SRC-18: D-025; SRC-20: §3.2]

The leading simplifications are:

- reuse the three-stage Selective Quality topology;
- delete obsolete P0.7 macro→mini movement and status surfaces;
- use one canonical frozen intake/manifest and one job as the transaction unit;
- preserve a narrow dynamic CV surface over a stable baseline;
- keep portal handling isolated from the core until Step 1 explicitly decides the boundary;
- use deterministic source addressing and unrestricted source escape rather than lossy retrieval authority;
- keep personality and Luna/context preparation optional, off-safe, attributable and non-authoritative;
- calibrate `READY / CHECK / RERUN` against actual human repair cost;
- build a deterministic vertical slice and target-Windows rendering proof before broad semantic production.

## 1. Product contract and success criteria

### 1.1 Intended product

`[USER]` The framework serves one candidate across multiple future batches. It is not a general recruitment platform, multi-candidate SaaS or autonomous job agent.

Every human-approved job is a production target. The framework has no semantic GO/NO-GO or model-side rejection gate after approval. Stretch roles, title mismatch, sector transfer, nontraditional profile and functionally equivalent experience are reframed, truthfully flagged or exception-handled—not silently vetoed.

For a conforming job, the product must be:

- truthful and interview-defensible;
- materially vacancy-specific rather than keyword-swapped;
- strategically coherent around one dominant professional backbone;
- natural native Bokmål for the first release unless Step 1 verifies a broader language need;
- complete for every required core deliverable;
- machine- and human-auditable without exposing internal artifacts to the employer;
- renderable to usable DOCX/PDF;
- reviewable and submittable by the human within the intended workload envelope.

### 1.2 Quality objective

The primary objective is the strong-majority distribution captured by `Q_happy`, interpreted with the later target of roughly 30 strong packages from approximately 40. The architecture must protect strong packages from being weakened by tail-uniformity machinery.

Success is not “every job receives the same process outcome.” It is:

- high median and strong-majority package quality;
- low total human minutes per submitted package;
- cheap identification of localized issues and weak strategic tail;
- no systemic truth inflation;
- no hidden loss of jobs or static CV history;
- no automatic external action;
- a baseline simple enough to build under the current deadlines.

### 1.3 Core deliverables — unresolved boundary

`[OPEN: STEP1_FREEZE]` The leading P0.9 product candidate is:

```text
CORE:      tailored CV + tailored application
DOWNSTREAM: portal/form answers when an actual form requires them
EXCEPTION: CV-only, portal-only, non-Norwegian or structurally unusual jobs
```

This is strongly supported by user permission, estimated low portal prevalence and the simplification benefit, but the corpus does not contain a formal adoption or a complete real-vacancy inventory. Step 1 must explicitly choose it. Until then, the architecture should isolate portal handling so either branch can be adopted without contaminating every core schema.

### 1.4 Human and prompt envelope

- approximately 5–10 human hours/week for review and submission;
- approximately 10–20 total human minutes per submitted package;
- first-batch target 1–4 weeks, with some relevant deadlines under two weeks;
- approximately 20–25 normal model prompts for a ~40-job batch, with a sharply rising justification threshold above that;
- routine between-job human confirmations are undesirable.

## 2. Architectural and design principles

1. **Truth hard; persuasion aggressive within truth.** Claim ceilings come from sources, not model confidence or repetition.
2. **Employer-facing package first.** Internal artifacts earn existence only by protecting quality, truth, usability or human effort.
3. **Selective quality, not uniform rescue.** Weak tail is surfaced and selectively rerun or handled separately.
4. **One job is the semantic transaction.** Persist, validate and contain failure before moving to the next job.
5. **Models own semantics; code owns mechanics.** Code validates identity, structure, references, expected sets, limits, paths and rendering—not naturalness or semantic truth.
6. **Strict factual authority, elastic strategy.** Structured handoffs must not become semantic cages.
7. **Source escape is unconditional.** Indexes and summaries navigate; original authorities remain available.
8. **Delete obsolete surfaces before hardening.** Do not preserve P0.8.1 machinery for product branches Step 1 removes.
9. **Minimal authoritative core; derived views may be rich.** Regenerable indexes, status views and human renderings are cheaper than duplicated manually maintained truth.
10. **Visible bounded repair, never hidden retry.** Validators may support same-session repair; they never launch semantic model calls.
11. **Partial success and idempotency.** One failure cannot corrupt or erase good jobs; reruns and rerenders are targeted.
12. **Manual irreversible boundary.** No employer contact, profile mutation or submission.
13. **Experimental layers are off-core, fail-open and attributable.** Baseline works fully without personality or Luna.
14. **Measure before automating.** Human spot-checks precede similarity engines; local render tests precede document-engine redesign.
15. **Stop when marginal decision value is low.** Current deadlines make speculative abstraction unusually expensive.

## 3. End-to-end topology

### 3.1 Pre-run

1. Human selects vacancies and creates/approves one row per vacancy.
2. Candidate authorities are confirmed current.
3. Core/exception status, cluster, sequence, deadline and operator priority are recorded.
4. Step-1 product rules determine which deliverables are core and which are downstream/exception.

### 3.2 Deterministic intake and freeze

The intake tool must:

- validate unique IDs and source paths;
- preserve original vacancy artifacts and hashes;
- create normalized text only as a derived convenience view;
- run deterministic extraction sanity checks and require original PDF/image/table inspection when content appears missing, garbled or structurally ambiguous;
- validate employer/title/reference/language/deadline/channel facts available from source/operator intake;
- reconcile every approved job into exactly one expected state;
- freeze authority, policy, schema and render-asset versions/hashes;
- generate derived job context and, if adopted, a deterministic evidence index;
- refuse freeze when identity, source or expected-set invariants fail.

### 3.3 Semantic production

For each cluster and stage, the human manually starts one model chat. Jobs are processed sequentially. For each job:

```text
load current job inputs
-> produce current stage artifact
-> run deterministic validator
-> bounded repair/revalidate if allowed
-> persist success or explicit failure
-> continue to next independent job
```

A continuation chat begins by reconciling artifacts against the frozen run rather than trusting a manually advanced pointer.

### 3.4 Finalization and human action

The finalizer enumerates expected core jobs from the frozen intake/manifest, validates current final/review artifacts, merges the stable CV baseline with dynamic zones, fills approved Word masters, renders, verifies outputs and generates a derived queue grouped by `READY`, `CHECK`, `RERUN`, `FAILED` and `EXCEPTION`.

The human performs final factual/semantic approval, captures portal forms where needed, chooses rerun/drop/exception handling and submits manually.

## 4. Canonical candidate and source authorities

The only candidate fact authorities during a production run are:

- `Work_experience.md`;
- `Education.md`;
- `Other_high_value_information.md`.

They are immutable during the run. Any source-maintenance work happens before a new freeze, with explicit human approval. A research report, prior CV, personality profile, model memory or previous package may reveal a maintenance need but cannot silently alter production truth.

### 4.2 Derived candidate baseline

`CV_BASELINE.md` or its successor owns stable rendering/history scaffolding such as:

- formal employer and role chronology;
- dates;
- education chronology;
- language/static sections;
- known employment IDs;
- default render structure.

It is derived and cannot raise claim ceilings. If an authority source changes, the baseline must be regenerated/revalidated before a new run.

### 4.3 Vacancy authority

The original vacancy is primary authority for:

- stated requirements and responsibilities;
- employer and role terminology;
- deadline and reference;
- actual submission/channel/form constraints visible in the source;
- language.

Normalized vacancy text is never a silent replacement. When layout, tables, attachments or portal screens matter, the original source or human-captured form remains authoritative.

- personality guides interpretation and misread prevention, not facts;

## 5. Vacancy intake, batch organization and freeze

### 5.1 Human-editable batch authority

`[SYNTHESIS][OPEN: STEP1_FREEZE]` The leading intake form is one operator-editable table such as `batch_inventory.csv`, compiled into a frozen machine manifest. It should avoid overlapping `channel`, `letter_required` and `portal_required` representations.

Minimum useful fields:

```text
job_id
employer
title
unit
reference
deadline
vacancy_path
language
sector
platform
cluster_id
sequence
user_priority
core_or_exception
notes
```

If portal remains core, exact required outputs and captured field limits must be canonical, not inferred from platform averages. If portal is detached, core intake should not pretend portal completeness is part of core package readiness.

### 5.2 Clustering

`[NORMATIVE control]` Start from roughly five clusters × eight jobs, normal range 6–10. Cluster size is empirical, not sacred.

`[SYNTHESIS][OPEN]` Human-assigned clusters and sequence are the leading v1 choice because they:

- remove a model-generated planning stage;
- make frozen expected state explicit;
- avoid A/B/cluster-plan duplication;
- respect the user’s high-agency manual selection;
- are easy to revise between runs.

Clustering guidance:

- group where shared decision context genuinely helps;
- separate near-duplicate jobs when phrase convergence is likely;
- do not place one identity archetype so early that it anchors the whole cluster;
- use deadline/operator priority for production order where necessary;
- preserve every job as an independent transaction.

### 5.3 Freeze object

`[SYNTHESIS][OPEN]` The leading state design uses one immutable `run_manifest.json` (or functionally equivalent object) as the freeze authority. It fingerprints:

- three candidate authorities and baseline;
- original vacancies;
- chosen product/language/settings policy;
- stage instructions;
- schemas and validator versions;
- Word masters/template map/render configuration;
- expected jobs, clusters, sequence and exception classes;
- the employer-facing document date or explicit date policy for each job/run.

`[SYNTHESIS strong default]` A pure rerender must not silently change an employer-facing document date or other semantic content. Date changes require an explicit semantic/job update, not merely a new converter invocation.

The manifest stores facts and hashes, not semantic conclusions. It does not duplicate full authority content per job.

### 5.4 Static stale reporting

A changed input should produce deterministic stale scope, not automatic semantic execution. Leading classes:

| Class | Minimum affected scope |
|---|---|
| `SOURCE_TRUTH` | strategy, draft, final, review and render for affected jobs |
| `STRATEGY_POLICY` | strategy and all downstream semantic artifacts |
| `WRITER_POLICY` | draft, final, review and render |
| `FINAL_POLICY` | final, review and render |
| `RENDER_ASSET` | rendered artifacts only |
| `SCHEMA_VALIDATION` | revalidation/structural adaptation unless semantics changed |

This is a reportable static mapping, not a dependency engine.

## 6. Evidence and provenance

### 6.1 Evidence contract

Every material candidate evidence item should be addressable by:

- source file;
- exact heading/section or stable evidence ID;
- sufficient surrounding context to preserve qualifiers;
- lifecycle/claim-ceiling metadata only when the source explicitly supports it.

The system must not manufacture precision by inferring unsupported lifecycle or claim ceilings into the index.

### 6.2 Deterministic evidence index

`[SYNTHESIS][OPEN: STEP1_FREEZE]` The strongest context candidate is a generated, lossless structural index over stable candidate sources. Minimal fields:

```text
evidence_id
source_file
heading_path
section_hash
approx_size
explicit_lifecycle_label?        # source-derived only
explicit_claim_ceiling_marker?   # source-derived only
```

It must not:

- rank evidence for a vacancy;
- summarize away caveats;
- label evidence irrelevant;
- become a claim graph or vector store;
- prevent models from opening the source.

Its potential value is broader than retrieval: it can provide one coordinate system for Stage-1 selection, Stage-2/3 source escape, final evidence synchronization, audit and optional context experiments.

### 6.3 Final provenance

The final package’s `used_evidence_refs` must describe the evidence actually used after Stage 3. A reference validator can prove only that the address exists and resolves; semantic support remains a model/human judgment.

Optional observability fields may record:

- evidence opened outside an advisory prep artifact;
- material strategy change;
- upstream claim repair.

These are diagnostic. Zero “outside prep” references does not prove perfect recall; it may also indicate anchoring.

### 6.4 Requirement map

Internally map each material stated requirement to:

```text
SUPPORTED | ADJACENT | GAP | NOT_MATERIAL | UNKNOWN
```

with evidence refs, claim ceiling and intended package role. This supports public-sector qualification logic and ordinary hiring relevance without forcing checklist prose into the application.

## 7. Source retrieval and context assembly

### 7.1 Ordering protocol

For each job, contextual order should normally be:

1. original/normalized vacancy and deterministic identity;
2. provisional employer decision problem and requirement map;
3. candidate source navigation/index;
4. original factual sources as needed;
5. optional interpretive layers such as personality;
6. prior model artifacts only after a fresh job view where the stage requires it.

This is a debiasing **protocol**, not a security or cognitive-isolation boundary.

### 7.2 Progressive evidence aperture

Models should receive enough context to decide well without treating a lossy preselection as complete. The practical pattern is:

```text
job-local core context
+ stable source addresses
+ unrestricted targeted source escape
+ optional advisory projection
```

No “summary of a summary” becomes the factual carrier. A material employer claim must remain traceable to original candidate authority.

### 7.3 Job-local views

A derived `job_context` may contain:

- deterministic identity and source paths;
- requirement map or vacancy structure;
- relevant policy pointers;
- selected evidence refs after Stage 1;
- current settings;
- explicit non-authority notice.

It is regenerable and subordinate to the manifest and original sources.

## 8. Stage 1 — Decision Architecture

### 8.1 Role

Stage 1 answers:

> Which truthful candidate identity, evidence architecture, employer-decision argument and writing direction gives this vacancy the strongest expected interview case?

It is a strategy stage, not a miniature application writer.

### 8.2 Model and execution

Current control: Sol Max or the strongest available reasoning model in the chosen execution surface. Model names/effort are volatile implementation parameters; the durable role is “strongest decision architecture.”

One cluster per manually initiated chat; jobs processed one at a time and validated before the next.

### 8.3 Per-job read order

1. Read vacancy and identity first.
2. Form a provisional employer problem, decision tests and requirement structure before candidate conditioning.
3. Inspect candidate authority/navigation and open sources.
4. If personality is enabled, consult it only after evidence understanding.
5. Produce the strategy artifact.
6. Validate, repair/revalidate under the adopted bounded protocol, persist or fail explicitly.

### 8.4 Required semantic output

The strategy must establish:

- employer decision problem and likely selection logic;
- primary candidate identity and why it is the best truthful route;
- explicit CV headline hypothesis;
- shortlist thesis;
- two strongest complementary evidence bridges plus bounded support;
- requirement→evidence/gap map;
- likely objections and truthful reframes;
- specific motivation bridge;
- CV versus application allocation;
- dynamic CV emphasis;
- writer instructions and explicit “avoid” guidance;
- at least one counterfactual reserve when a materially different route is plausible;
- evidence refs and claim ceilings;
- `ELASTIC` final-writer freedom;
- personality resonance only if enabled/useful.

Stage 1 must not assign a numeric fit probability, veto an approved job, invent facts, draft the full employer package or forbid later source escape.

## 9. Strategy representation and authority

### 9.1 Semantic contract is primary

`[OPEN: STEP1_FREEZE]` Serialization remains one of the most consequential unresolved choices. The final architecture should first freeze a semantic contract and then choose a representation.

Four consumers exist:

1. Terra writer;
2. final Sol;
3. deterministic reference/structure validation;
4. human audit.

A good representation must serve them without duplicating truth or inflating upstream authority.

### 9.2 Required epistemic typing

Regardless of Markdown or JSON, the artifact must communicate:

```text
artifact_role = STRATEGY_HYPOTHESIS
authority = NON_FACTUAL_NON_FINAL
elasticity = ELASTIC
final_writer_may_reconstruct = true
```

A schema-valid strategy is not a final decision and cannot raise claim ceilings.

### 9.3 Current options

**Control — stable-label Markdown**

- historically normative in P0.8;
- naturally supports employer reasoning;
- human-readable;
- harder to validate deeply without brittle parsing;
- may allow important evidence/ref fields to drift.

**Leading challenger — structured decision core + prose-capable fields**

- mechanically validates IDs, refs, enums and required decision surfaces;
- can generate a human-readable view deterministically;
- risks generic field-filling and formalization-authority leakage;
- manual Work/Codex output still needs a local validator; API Structured Outputs guarantees do not apply automatically.

**Avoid — duplicated JSON plus independently authored Markdown.** That creates dual truth.

### 9.4 Candidate semantic shape

```text
job identity
artifact role / non-authority / elasticity
employer decision model
candidate identity + avoid identity
headline hypothesis
material requirements and candidate relation
evidence plan with refs, roles and ceilings
objections/reframes
motivation bridge
CV direction
application direction
counterfactual reserve
gaps/risks
optional personality resonance
source-escape notes
```

### 9.5 Length

P0.8 control is roughly 900–1400 words, soft maximum around 1800. Later synthesis suggests 400–1000 for lower anchoring and higher decision density. This remains open. There should be no arbitrary minimum; the correct target is enough reasoning to make the writer independent, not a verbose essay.

### 9.6 Falsification criterion

A small comparison is warranted only if the format remains load-bearing. The failure signal for structure is not merely malformed JSON; it is lower final quality, generic field-filling, worse human inspectability or reduced willingness of Stage 3 to reconstruct weak strategy.

## 10. Stage 2 — Native Package Writer

### 10.1 Role

Stage 2 realizes the strategy as a complete native Norwegian package. It owns coherent prose, voice, channel complementarity and first editorial self-correction. It is not a mechanical template filler and not a separate judge.

### 10.2 Model and inputs

Current control: Terra Max or the strongest cost-effective native-writing model that preserves quality.

Per job, Stage 2 receives:

- vacancy and deterministic identity;
- current strategy;
- writing and persuasion standards;
- targeted candidate evidence/source addresses;
- dynamic CV/baseline contract;
- distilled selected personality guidance only when enabled;
- no raw Luna preparation by default.

It may reopen original authorities when the strategy is insufficient or ambiguous.

### 10.3 Writer freedom

Follow the strategy strongly but not mechanically. If an instruction is contradictory, unwriteable or clearly weak, Stage 2 may make a bounded correction and record a short writer delta. The delta is not a competing strategy object.

### 10.4 Draft artifact

The draft package contains only dynamic content and internal provenance, conceptually:

```json
{
  "job_id": "...",
  "baseline_version": "...",
  "used_evidence_refs": ["..."],
  "cv_overrides": {
    "headline": "...",
    "profile": "...",
    "key_competencies": ["..."],
    "employment_overrides": [
      {"employment_id": "...", "bullets": ["..."]}
    ]
  },
  "application": {"text_or_sections": "..."},
  "portal_answers": "conditional on Step-1 boundary",
  "writer_delta": null
}
```

The exact schema should avoid copying static factual scaffolding owned by the baseline.

### 10.5 Completion

Stage 2 persists a validator-clean draft or an explicit failure. It never marks completion merely because a filename or heading exists.

## 11. CV tailoring and generation

### 11.1 Stable versus dynamic ownership

`[NORMATIVE control][USER permission]` The leading v1 surface is deliberately narrow:

| Baseline/source-owned | Model-authored dynamic |
|---|---|
| employer names, formal titles, dates, education chronology, languages, static sections, employment IDs | headline, profile, competency selection/order/wording, employment bullet sets |

This creates substantial vacancy-specific positioning without allowing the model to regenerate the candidate’s chronology.

### 11.2 Explicit headline

P0.7 lacked a dedicated headline and indirectly derived identity from competencies. P0.8’s explicit headline is a direct quality improvement and should remain.

The headline must:

- express the primary candidate identity chosen for this vacancy;
- be supportable by factual authorities;
- avoid unsupported seniority or formal role inflation;
- align with, but not mechanically copy, the vacancy title;
- remain consistent with CV profile and application backbone.

### 11.3 Employment override semantics

`[P0.8.1 strong default]` When an `employment_id` is overridden, the dynamic bullet array is a **full replacement** of that entry’s rendered default/dynamic bullets.

Forbidden ambiguity:

- append model bullets to hidden baseline bullets;
- positional merge;
- mixed old/new bullet set without explicit rule;
- empty array silently meaning “use defaults.”

The baseline still owns employer/title/period and always-preserved chronology.

### 11.4 CV doctrine

Dynamic CV content should:

- foreground evidence most diagnostic for the employer’s decision;
- use compact action/contribution/consequence language;
- make functional leadership visible without inventing formal management authority;
- separate personal and team contribution accurately;
- preserve lifecycle distinctions such as pilot versus production;
- avoid keyword dumping and generic competence lists;
- preserve readable page density.

### 11.5 Page behavior

- sparse CV may be one page;
- normal/dense CV is typically two pages;
- >2 pages should normally produce a bounded review issue rather than automatic semantic deletion;
- hidden typography auto-fit is not part of the baseline;
- representative sparse/normal/dense render fixtures are required.

## 12. Application-letter generation

The application should be a coherent employer decision argument, not a prose duplicate of the CV or a checklist of every requirement.

Normal structure:

1. open on the employer’s actual problem/opportunity and the relevant candidate route;
2. establish one primary identity/backbone;
3. develop two or three evidence-rich proof arcs;
4. connect past evidence to future employer usefulness;
5. express motivation through problem/work fit rather than generic enthusiasm;
6. include at least one evidence-grounded interview hook: a concrete problem, delivery or judgment area that naturally invites useful follow-up;
7. address a material gap or objection honestly where useful;
8. close with calm, concrete contribution logic.

As a default rhetorical heuristic—not a mechanical quota—roughly 70–85% of meaningful content should serve the top one or two employer pain points and the two strongest evidence bridges; roughly 15–30% may serve motivation, framing, controlled future contribution and closing. Unequal evidence should not receive equal space. The CV carries breadth and screening coverage; the application concentrates the hiring argument.

A useful internal transformation is:

```text
raw fact
-> employer latent need or risk
-> strategic employer-value angle
-> channel role
-> natural Norwegian sentence
```

Do not expose this matrix mechanically through repetitive “Dette er relevant fordi”, identical problem–action–result formulas, dramatic fear language or advertisement-order paragraphs.

Language doctrine:

- generate directly in Bokmål;
- use ordinary Norwegian word order and precise action verbs;
- prefer work, responsibility, scope, judgment and consequence over self-ranking adjectives;
- use `jeg` for supported personal contribution and `vi` for collective delivery;
- avoid vague “structured, analytical, solution-oriented” claims without evidence;
- avoid American corporate hype, cliché enthusiasm and translation artifacts;
- preserve the candidate’s plausible voice.

Review or remove stock phrases unless the surrounding evidence makes them unusually precise: `brenner for`, `lidenskapelig opptatt av`, `unik mulighet`, `spennende reise`, `fremoverlent`, `resultatorientert`, `dynamisk`, `synergieffekter`, `game changer`, `revolusjonerende`, `sømløst`, `banebrytende`, `utnytte kraften i`, `jeg er overbevist om at`, and generic `i skjæringspunktet mellom`. The governing rule is not lexical prohibition for its own sake: move strength from self-descriptive adjectives into supported work, responsibility, scope, judgment and consequence.

Length is a soft editorial target unless the vacancy/form imposes a hard limit. The historical one-page convention remains useful, but final layout should not delete decision-relevant content to satisfy an arbitrary proxy.

## 13. Stage 3 — Elastic Final Writer and Selective Review

### 13.1 Role

Stage 3 is the final semantic quality stage, not an independent external judge. It must:

1. reconstruct or repair the package into the strongest truth-safe final form;
2. classify the remaining repair scope for human action.

### 13.2 Read-order protocol

For each job:

1. read vacancy/identity first and form a fresh provisional view;
2. inspect relevant candidate evidence/source navigation;
3. then read strategy and draft;
4. source-escape whenever needed;
5. consult personality only if active/relevant;
6. create final package;
7. synchronize evidence refs;
8. create review object;
9. validate and repair/revalidate before persistence.

This is a debiasing protocol, not proof of independence.

### 13.3 Elastic reconstruction authority

`[USER LOCKED]` Stage 3 may:

- change headline and primary identity;
- replace evidence selection;
- switch to a counterfactual strategy;
- change employment emphasis and bullets;
- rewrite profile, competencies and entire application;
- change argument sequence and channel role;
- suppress personality-conditioned framing;
- reopen evidence omitted upstream.

It may not:

- invent support;
- exceed claim ceilings;
- mutate factual authorities;
- ignore employer hard constraints;
- treat validator success as proof of semantic truth.

### 13.4 Final semantic artifacts

The final package uses the same core semantic shape as the draft so rendering has one source contract. It becomes the current model-authored package authority for the job, subordinate to candidate/vacancy facts and human approval.

The review artifact should contain:

- `READY | CHECK | RERUN`;
- no more than three concise concrete reasons/action notes;
- known limitations requiring human attention;
- optional `material_strategy_change` and summary;
- optional `upstream_claim_repaired` and bounded note;
- optional outside-prep evidence refs when a prep condition exists.

No numeric confidence or self-awarded quality score is required.

## 14. Strategy reconstruction and correction

A central P0.9 invariant is that upstream formalization must not become final semantic authority.

Mechanisms that support this:

- strategy explicitly typed as hypothesis;
- `ELASTIC` final-writer contract;
- vacancy-first Stage-3 read order;
- unrestricted source escape;
- counterfactual reserve in strategy where meaningful;
- observable material strategy change;
- final evidence synchronization;
- human final authority.

`material_strategy_change` is useful for architecture evaluation:

- high appropriate change may show Stage 3 is exercising necessary freedom;
- high change caused by recurrent Stage-1 weakness may indicate a strategy-contract problem;
- near-zero change is ambiguous—it may indicate excellent strategy or authority inflation;
- the metric must be interpreted with final quality and human notes.

An upstream unsupported claim must be repaired before final save, not left in the package with a `CHECK` label.

## 15. Validation layers

### 15.1 Mechanical hard validation

Deterministic code should enforce what is actually deterministic:

- schema/parse validity;
- job/run/employer/title identity equality;
- unique IDs and expected-set equality;
- known employment IDs;
- evidence-ref syntax and resolvability;
- required artifacts/fields;
- exact employer-imposed portal limits when portal output exists;
- path safety and allowed roots/file types;
- template mapping keys;
- placeholder/transient-control leakage;
- expected output existence, nonzero size and openability;
- baseline merge completeness;
- no accidental private artifact in employer-facing output;
- target rerender/idempotency invariants.

### 15.2 Semantic hard policy

Models and human enforce:

- no fabricated or inflated facts;
- evidence supports wording and contribution attribution;
- formal versus functional authority is honest;
- pilot/current/production lifecycle is represented correctly;
- gaps are not disguised as direct experience;
- motivation facts are not invented.

A Python keyword scan may catch a high-precision known leak, but cannot certify these semantics.

### 15.3 Soft quality and review

Models/human evaluate:

- employer relevance;
- one-backbone coherence;
- evidence selection;
- native Norwegian naturalness;
- persuasion;
- distinctiveness;
- redundancy;
- page density and visual balance;
- motivation strength;
- cross-job convergence.

These may trigger editorial repair, `CHECK`, `RERUN` or later calibration. They are not hard schema failures unless an employer imposes an exact limit.

### 15.4 Protocol versus boundary taxonomy

Every control should be typed honestly:

- `BOUNDARY` — mechanically enforceable, e.g. write roots, schema, immutable source hash;
- `PROTOCOL` — instructed behavior, e.g. vacancy-first read order;
- `ADVISORY` — optional context or heuristic;
- `HUMAN_GATE` — final semantic/irreversible decision.

Tests must target the correct type. A prompt containing “read vacancy first” does not prove the protocol worked.

## 16. READY / CHECK / RERUN / FAILED semantics

`READY / CHECK / RERUN` are operator-facing repair predictions, not objective grades.

### READY

No known material issue beyond normal human skim/fact check. Submission still requires human approval.

### CHECK

A small number of localized issues or confirmations that the human can handle without rebuilding the backbone, such as:

- a claim-strength nuance;
- preference-sensitive wording;
- a narrow employer detail requiring confirmation;
- localized layout/page issue;
- a small motivation ambiguity.

### RERUN

Strategic reconstruction is more efficient than human editing, such as:

- wrong candidate identity;
- weak/mismatched primary evidence;
- broken application backbone;
- serious genericity/convergence;
- multi-section rewrite;
- uncertainty too broad for a bounded human check.

### FAILED

Deterministic runtime state: the required current stage artifacts could not be made validator-clean under the adopted bounded repair protocol. `FAILED` is not Stage 3’s subjective quality judgment.

### Delivery classes

The finalizer may derive:

```text
DELIVERED_READY
DELIVERED_CHECK
DELIVERED_RERUN
FAILED
EXCEPTION
```

A nonzero run-level status must not delete or hide valid delivered jobs.

## 17. Failure, repair and rerun behavior

### 17.1 Bounded same-session repair

`[OPEN attempt count; strong default = 2]`

```text
validator failure
-> model reads localized deterministic error
-> edits current artifact in same chat
-> revalidates
-> after bounded unsuccessful attempts: record failure, do not complete, continue safely
```

Validator code never launches the model. Actual same-session behavior must be proven on the selected Work/Codex surface.

### 17.2 RERUN

Leading complete contract:

- manually initiated;
- one job;
- separate Sol-class chat;
- current vacancy, authorities, strategy, draft, final, review and relevant history available;
- full reconstruction allowed within truth;
- replacement final/review validated before becoming current;
- old versions preserved in bounded history;
- never a standard fourth stage for every job.

### 17.3 Render repair

Rendering is deterministic and may be rerun independently after a template/font/converter fix. A rerender does not require semantic stages unless the semantic package itself changes.

### 17.4 Exception jobs

Rare non-core language/channel structures remain in the inventory with an explicit exception class. They can be manually handled or routed through a deliberately separate extension; they do not block conforming core jobs.

## 18. Document, template and rendering architecture

### 18.1 Evidence-based reuse posture

`[VERIFIED]` P0.7’s actual masters are more favorable than generic “avoid content controls” warnings imply:

- CV master: 17 plain, non-data-bound SDTs; no body tables, text boxes or drawings; linear body; name/“CV” in header;
- application master: 7 SDT instances/6 tags; no text boxes/drawings; two tables; contact information in header;
- P0.7 finalizer filled OOXML controls, unwrapped them before LibreOffice and failed if any `w:sdt` remained;
- controlled fixture DOCX/PDF rendering passed.

This supports inspection/adaptation rather than automatic rejection. It does **not** prove target-Windows headless behavior, current font availability, portal parsing or header extraction.

### 18.2 Template contract

There must be one canonical package→template source map. Code and documentation may not maintain competing mappings.

The finalizer should:

1. validate current final package and review;
2. merge stable baseline + dynamic CV zones;
3. fill CV/application masters;
4. remove transient controls/placeholders under the chosen renderer technique;
5. validate DOCX structure;
6. write outputs to a temporary job transaction path;
7. convert required PDFs under isolated converter state;
8. validate each expected output independently;
9. atomically promote the successful job transaction;
10. preserve prior valid outputs if a replacement fails.

### 18.3 Target-environment verification

Before real production:

- install/locate converter;
- test `soffice --headless` or chosen alternative on target Windows;
- use isolated writable profile when appropriate;
- verify absolute paths/quoting;
- verify fonts and Norwegian glyphs;
- inspect header/footer preservation;
- render sparse/normal/dense CV and letter/no-letter fixtures;
- check page count, placeholders, openability and selectable/extractable text;
- visually inspect clipping, overflow, blank space, page breaks and font substitution;
- test targeted rerender and failed-conversion containment.

### 18.4 LibreOffice process policy

Architecture invariant: **job transaction, independently verified outputs**.

Implementation parameter:

- one isolated invocation for both documents if target testing proves reliable;
- otherwise one isolated invocation per document.

No persistent LibreOffice daemon is justified in P0.9.

### 18.5 ATS/parseability posture

Do not redesign from generic ATS folklore. Use conservative structure and test actual outputs:

- logical reading order;
- normal text for critical content;
- no data-bound controls surviving final output;
- avoid critical facts solely in decorative objects;
- selectable/extractable text;
- inspect whether name/contact in headers is reliably parsed;
- actual vacancy/platform constraints override market generalities.

## 19. Portal and channel boundary

### 19.1 Leading detached candidate

`[SYNTHESIS][OPEN]` The strongest simplification candidate is a downstream portal workflow that begins after the current core CV/application package exists.

Platform behavior and attachment/field limits are employer/vacancy-specific. The architecture must capture actual fields rather than hardcode “Webcruiter rules.” Public-sector qualification principles justify explicit internal requirement coverage, not mechanically checklist-like letter prose.

- job-local derived view;

This reduces authority leakage and helps downstream models use rich context without confusing it with truth.

## 22. Personality context

### 22.1 Locked boundary

`[USER]` If personality exists in runtime, it is optional and controlled through settings. The pipeline must work completely with it off.

The canonical profile is interpretive context. It may guide:

- evidence search and selection;
- identity framing;
- motivation/authenticity;
- misread prevention;
- tone and candidate specificity.

It may not create achievements, responsibilities, skills, results, work history or claim authority.

### 22.2 Current source value

The profile provides high-signal facets such as systems mapping, option generation, evidence loops, craft/integrity standards, governance-based delegation, rapid domain learning, leverage/tool-building, cognitive empathy and direct communication, plus explicit counterweights such as scope expansion and social-temperature risk.

These are useful only when bound to factual candidate evidence and vacancy relevance.

### 22.3 Runtime candidate

A bounded candidate behavior is:

1. Stage 1 understands vacancy and factual evidence first.
2. It selects zero to a few relevant facets only when useful.

5. Stage 3 may suppress or reconstruct all personality-conditioned content.

The exact 20 PF IDs and “0–3” count are useful proposals, not user-adopted architecture.

### 22.4 Interim and evaluation

- total package quality;

- evidence defensibility;

- human edits/time.

## 26. Pilot and evaluation

### 26.1 Three evidence loops

**A — engineering proof:** ingest/freeze/validate/merge/render/reconcile work correctly. No model-quality inference.

**B — clean product baseline:** core Sol→Terra→Sol with experimental modules off; observe quality and human cost.

**C — causal feature tests:** one uncertain mechanism at a time, such as personality, strategy format or Luna prep.

### 26.2 Pre-production gates

1. authority and final architecture frozen;
2. actual vacancy inventory/product boundary resolved;
3. target template/converter preflight;
4. one-job deterministic vertical slice with synthetic valid/invalid artifacts;
5. two real-job live smoke through the chosen model surfaces;
6. only then broader cluster pilot.

### 26.3 Eight-job pilot

Use approximately one representative cluster. Capture per job:

- human 1–10 overall quality;
- submit-ready yes/no;
- factual correction needed;
- total human handling time;
- review status and actual repair scope;
- Stage-3 material change;
- cluster position;
- rerun need/result;
- notable convergence or context failure.

For `n=8`, report median, mean all, mean top 6, sensitivity mean top 7, explicit `Q_happy` definition and bottom-two details. Do not imply statistical precision.

### 26.4 Calibration

Historical application packages and interview conversion are not comparable baselines. Use the first current batch to establish strong/medium/weak examples and update persuasion calibration only from recent comparable evidence.

Review calibration questions:

- Are low-quality packages labeled READY?
- Are CHECK items actually local?
- Are RERUN items fixable only through strategic rewrite?
- How many strong packages are over-escalated?
- What is the real human time by state?

### 26.5 Convergence and ATS checks

Use human spot-checks and representative extraction first. Build automation only if recurrent failures demonstrate marginal value.

## 28. Acceptance criteria

### 28.1 Architecture

- product boundary and required outputs unambiguous;
- one factual authority layer;
- one canonical run freeze;
- explicit authority/epistemic type for every artifact;
- three-stage role split and final elasticity clear;
- no hidden semantic orchestration;

- unique job IDs;
- original vacancy preserved/hashed;

- cluster/sequence/priority canonical;

- expected set validates.

- correct identity;
- validator-clean structure;
- resolvable evidence refs;
- dynamic CV only in allowed zones;
- full replacement employment semantics;

- final evidence refs synchronized;

- continuation reconciles artifacts;
- failed job cannot erase good jobs;
- finalizer enumerates frozen expectations;
- targeted rerun/rerender works;
- stale inputs are reported;

- expected DOCX/PDF generated and openable;
- no placeholders/transient controls;
- no broken glyphs/clipping in representative QA;
- text extractable/selectable where expected;

### 28.6 Product pilot

- packages materially vacancy-specific;
- truth inflation absent/systematically rare and corrected;
- Stage 3 reconstructs weak upstream work when necessary;
- human time broadly compatible with 10–20 minutes/submitted package;
- review statuses roughly match repair scope;
- no severe early/late cluster degradation or cross-job convergence;
- weak tail is identifiable without degrading the strong majority.

No single numeric score substitutes for judgment.
