MarTech Specialist

As a MarTech Specialist, you will work in the MarTech Platform team to build, develop, and
optimize Strawberry's marketing technology platform. You will gather technical and functional
business requirements, configure platform components to support marketing strategies across
the organization, and coordinate with stakeholders.

You will contribute directly to platform development, system integrations, and the lifecycle of
connected marketing tools. This role requires hands-on technical work and close collaboration
with developers, architects, and business stakeholders.

Our current MarTech stack includes:

• Braze - Customer Engagement Platform
• mParticle - Customer Data Platform
• Dynamic Yield - Personalization Engine
• Contentful - Content Management System

MAIN TASKS / KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

• Develop and configure MarTech solutions to support marketing and business objectives.
• Collaborate with business stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into
technical solutions.
• Partner with solution architects and integration developers to ensure robust, scalable
platform architecture.
• Ensure all MarTech components comply with data governance, privacy, and security
standards.
• Maintain and enhance personalization capabilities across marketing channels.
• Provide technical support and guidance to internal marketing teams.
• Create and maintain documentation for configurations, workflows, and platform best
practices.

ROLE PROFILE / WE ARE LOOKING FOR A PERSON WITH

• 3+ years of hands-on experience in a MarTech, Marketing Operations, or similar technical
role.
• Experience with platforms such as:
◦ Engagement Platforms (e.g., Braze, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, HubSpot)
◦ CDPs (e.g., mParticle, Segment, Tealium)
