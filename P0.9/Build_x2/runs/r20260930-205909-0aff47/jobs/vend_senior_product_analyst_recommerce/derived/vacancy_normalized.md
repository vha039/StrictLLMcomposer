WHAT'S THE JOB LIKE? Day-to-day work & projects cooking

As a Senior Product Analyst in ReCommerce, you help our product teams build the right features
faster and learn from them reliably.

You work closely with Product Managers, UX Designers, Developers, and fellow Analysts to:

• Design and interpret experiments that connect product changes to commercial outcomes -
including revenue, liquidity, and marketplace health.
• Build reusable measurement foundations: cohort models, segmentation frameworks, and
KPI definitions that the broader team can draw on repeatedly.
• Run deep-dive analyses into category dynamics, user behavior, and competitive
positioning across our Nordic marketplaces.
• Define success metrics and guardrails for product initiatives, including AI-driven features
where measurement discipline is crucial.
• Create clear dashboards and reports that inform both day-to-day decisions and strategic
direction.

Our ReCommerce Insights & Analytics team spans Sweden, Norway, Denmark, and Finland. We
combine deep product work with cross-team collaboration, knowledge sharing, and increasingly,
AI-augmented tooling.

Success after 6 months? You've built strong relationships with your product teams,
contributed to experimentation and measurement for key initiatives, and delivered analyses that
moved decisions forward.

Success after 1 year? You're a trusted analytical partner for marketplace performance and
experimentation, with reusable models and frameworks that increase the leverage of the entire
team.
