Shape our data governance

Are you passionate about turning data into a strategic asset and enabling
responsible AI adoption? Hydro is looking for a Data Governance Lead to drive our global
data governance agenda and establish the foundations for trusted data, analytics, and AI across
the organization.

In this role, you will work closely with business and technology stakeholders to ensure clear data
ownership, strong governance practices, and high-quality data that support value creation,
compliance, and innovation across Hydro.

What we offer you

• The opportunity to shape Hydro's global data governance capabilities and support
responsible AI adoption.
• A key role in enabling data-driven decision-making, analytics, and digital transformation.
• High degree of autonomy and flexibility in an international environment.
• Opportunities for professional growth, strategic impact, and broad stakeholder exposure.
• A culture that encourages innovation, learning, and cross-functional collaboration.

What you will be doing

• Data governance leadership: Establish and implement Hydro's data governance
framework, policies, and operating model. Define decision rights, data ownership,
stewardship responsibilities, and accountability across the organization.
