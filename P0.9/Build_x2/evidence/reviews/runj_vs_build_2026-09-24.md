# Build_x2 runj compared with later Build packages

**Review date:** 2026-09-24. **Verdict:** Build_x2 produced cleaner, more
predictable documents, but this batch does not demonstrate higher interview
value than Build. The vacancies differ, and the new batch contains several
roles with explicit requirements the candidate cannot document. Do not use
the small difference in editorial scores as a promotion gate.

## Scope and method

The new run is
`P0.9/Build_x2/runs/r20260923-193222-838ddd` (cluster `runj`):
seven finalized CORE CV/letter pairs and one Nynorsk EXCEPTION, Bufdir, with
no package. Comparators are the four most recent completed Build runs:

- `r20260920-212518-413d77`: Tolletaten, SpareBank 1;
- `r20260915-190926-020eaa`: Itera;
- `r20260914-211651-5dbd38`: Stortinget, Norwegian, EIE, Abacus;
- `r20260914-211650-5d3516`: Tieto, BearingPoint, Amesto, Visma.

For each package, I checked the original vacancy and normalized extraction,
the frozen candidate authority, semantic final package, rendered PDF when
present, and render report. I visually inspected contact sheets of the actual
PDF pages and checked page counts, extracted text and PDF hash matches.
The seven x2 packages and ten of eleven Build comparators have PDFs; Itera
has DOCX only. The frozen WORK/EDU/OTHER/CV_BASELINE and settings hashes are
identical across these five runs. Vacancies, human/model sessions and dates
differ; there is no matched A/B or interview outcome.

**Score:** editorial package execution out of 100: truthful, attributable and
interview-defensible claims (20); material vacancy answers and honest gaps
(25); specific contribution, adoption and lifecycle (20); employer argument
and motivation (15); readable Bokmål and tone (10); actual physical package
(10). Fit is a separate subjective 1–5 rating of the candidate against the
vacancy; it is not a score for the writing. Scores are judgment estimates,
with roughly ±5 points of resolution. An unknown physical score stays
unknown rather than receiving an invented midpoint.

## Cross-run findings

1. **Presentation improved.** X2 has seven of seven complete pairs within
   2/1 pages, versus six of ten PDF-complete Build pairs. X2's combined
   `SPRÅK OG KURS` section and omitted references avoid the almost-empty
   third page visible in three Build CVs. X2 headlines are one line in the
   inspected PDFs. This is the clearest demonstrated improvement.
2. **Truth remains strong, while selection is uneven.** I found no material
   fabricated experience in the checked text. The best x2 letters (Bærum,
   Würth) use concrete, appropriately bounded transfer arguments. However,
   `READY` on all seven x2 finals should not be read as eligibility:
   Trifid's seven-year threshold, Nittedal's degree requirement, Politiet's
   all-requirements condition, KPMG's 5–8 years and NAV's direct ResearchOps
   requirements remain material gaps. The best Build packages (Abacus, EIE,
   Stortinget, Tolletaten) are at least as convincing on their own vacancies.
3. **One editorial objective missed.** The semantic CV text has no first
   person in 0/7 x2 packages versus 5/11 Build packages, as intended. But all
   seven x2 letters use a variant of “Jeg forteller gjerne … i et intervju”;
   only two of eleven Build comparators use that phrase. The x2
   `stock_close` regex currently detects “ser frem til …”, not this repeated
   formula. Thus the new diagnostic does not surface the batch-wide tic.
4. **Three x2 status results now say render FAILED because CV PDFs were
   renamed after rendering.** NAV and Bærum have `CV_Vegard_Haaland.pdf`;
   Trifid has `CV_KI_governance.pdf`. Each actual file's SHA-256 matches
   the corresponding render report's PDF hash and is a readable two-page
   document; the report's old `output_path` no longer resolves. Derived x2
   status therefore shows render OK 4 / FAILED 3, despite the physical PDFs
   existing. Five of the older Build comparator CV PDFs were likewise
   renamed after rendering. Treat this as a locator/receipt mismatch, not
   proof of failed conversion. Restore the reported names or rerender through
   the intended workflow before relying on status; do not silently edit the
   receipts.

## Decision

X2's compact CV template and first-person restraint warrant keeping for
further paired testing. This run **does not establish superior interview
likelihood or justify promotion over Build**: the same-vacancy control arms
specified in `evaluation/PROTOCOL.md` were not run, and the current x2
cohort has several hard or near-hard qualification gaps. The most useful
next review is a same-vacancy blind comparison after the renamed-PDF status
discrepancy is reconciled. Independently, revise the editorial prompt or
diagnostic if closing variation matters.
